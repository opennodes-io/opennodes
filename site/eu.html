An open way to find and verify Europe's AI inference

OpenNodes is an open protocol and an open-source registry for finding and verifying AI inference endpoints — from a university GPU cluster or an AI Factory service to a regional cloud. Providers publish a signed self-description; registries check those claims automatically; clients call the provider directly. OpenNodes is never in the data path.

The European gap

Europe is building inference capacity in many places at once — EuroHPC AI Factories and Antennas, national centres, sovereign and regional clouds — but there is no open, vendor-neutral way to discover and compare it. The usual shortcut is a commercial broker in the middle of every request: prompts pass through a third party, often outside the EEA, and trust rests on the broker's own claims. ONP standardizes discovery and verification as an open layer, and leaves invocation (OpenAI-compatible), tools (MCP) and payment to existing standards.

How it maps to European requirements

Keep prompts with the chosen processor (GDPR, transfer minimisation)Discovery is metadata only; clients call the provider directly. The hosted registry never receives prompts (one documented exception: the web advisor box).Live
Choose providers by jurisdictionRegistered nodes declare the operator's country in their signed card; for imported listings of known European providers it is inferred and labeled as such. The registry also checks where each server is (IP geolocation and network, round-trip times from Nuremberg and Helsinki) and drops a server found outside the EEA from the filter — evidence, not proof.Live

What it is not: OpenNodes does not certify compliance with the AI Act or GDPR. It makes the facts deployers need visible, signed and checkable; the obligations stay with providers and deployers.

Where it could fit

AI Factories & Antennas

A common discovery and verification layer for the inference services they expose — find, compare and call them through one open interface. Reference fingerprints for open-weight models could be computed on EuroHPC systems.

Sovereign & regional clouds

A free, neutral channel to be found and verified, with measured performance and disclosed hardware, without a broker in the traffic or taking a cut.

Data spaces (Gaia-X / Simpl)

Signed self-descriptions plus verification are close in spirit to Gaia-X self-descriptions; a mapping is future work.

European models

The registry lists European models wherever providers serve them — Mistral (370+ offerings) and Poland's Bielik today — and the EU filter keeps traffic with EEA operators. Our Finnish reference node serves Bielik 1.5B v3 (SpeakLeash, Apache-2.0), fully verified: identity matched and the whole 8,192-token context passed retrieval tests.

Status today

  • Hosted registry at registry.opennodes.io (Hetzner, Nuremberg, Germany): ~10,000 offerings from ~250 providers imported from public catalogs — shown as unverified until claimed — plus verified reference nodes in Germany (Llama 3.2 1B) and Finland (Bielik 1.5B v3, a Polish open model). This website is served by Cloudflare.
  • Open packages: MCP server and OpenAI-compatible gateway, Ollama router, self-hostable registry, and a Python kit for operators; listed in the official MCP Registry.
  • An independent open-source project (spec CC-BY-4.0, code Apache-2.0); a European legal entity is planned for commercial agreements and programme participation.
