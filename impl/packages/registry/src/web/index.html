Register an AI node

Anyone can register — a lab, a university cluster, a single Ollama box. Registration is open; trust is earned through the automated qualification procedure. Serve a signed Node Card at /.well-known/open-node.json (the Node Kit does this for any vLLM/Ollama/TGI deployment), then submit it here.

The qualification procedure

Listing is open; tiers are earned. The registry runs an automated admission battery against your live endpoints — no paperwork, no gatekeepers, no exceptions. Every verdict is recorded and visible in your node's listing history.

STAGE AConformance — gate to COMMUNITY, minutes. Health endpoint live; card valid and signed; models list agrees with the card; one blind invocation returns proper usage accounting and a signed receipt. Re-run every 6 hours and on every card revision.
STAGE BSubstance — gate to VERIFIED, hours. Identity fingerprinting: a deterministic probe battery compared against reference outputs of the claimed model — serving a cheaper substitute under a premium name marks the node disputed and delists it. Context verification: needle-in-haystack probes at 25/50/90% of the claimed window; overclaims are capped to the verified size, visibly. Capacity ramp: stepped concurrent load; measured TTFT/throughput overrides claims in search results.
STAGE CContinuous — keeps the tier honest. Liveness sweeps feed a rolling availability score; random blind invocations at unpredictable times defeat "benchmark mode"; sustained failures auto-suspend the listing.

Principles

Claimed ≠ measured ≠ attested. Self-reported values are labeled CLAIMED; registry measurements override them and show as ● MEASURED; third-party Verifiable Credentials earn attested. The three are never blended.

Transparent rank, by rule. Every result carries its rank explanation — component weights and values. Undisclosed ranking inputs are non-conformant; sponsored placement, if ever present, must be labeled and excluded from rank.

Probes cost you nothing you don't grant. Your card declares a probe allowance; the registry stays within it or pays like any client.
